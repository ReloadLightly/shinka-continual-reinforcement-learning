<DESCRIPTION>
Use centroid fitness more strongly so an exceptional individual cannot prematurely suppress exploration. Gate exploration by archive-relative offspring competitiveness, while retaining the archive-loss response. Contract faster when the archive substantially outperforms the population, indicating disruptive mutations. These feedback rules aim to improve acquisition and centroid quality without relying on task timing; their benefit requires evaluation.
</DESCRIPTION>
