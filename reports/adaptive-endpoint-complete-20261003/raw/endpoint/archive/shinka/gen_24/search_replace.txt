Replace the alternating-probe gradient estimate with a smoothed stagnation signal. Generation-to-generation fitness changes also reflect selection and population noise, so attributing them to probe direction can mislead width adaptation. Instead, pull mutation width toward a bounded target that increases when quality is low and progress stalls, while reducing exploration when the archive outperforms the population. Preserve decline-triggered recovery and reduce probe amplitude to limit disruption. This rule uses only observed feedback and persistent memory; its effect on combined_score requires evaluation.
