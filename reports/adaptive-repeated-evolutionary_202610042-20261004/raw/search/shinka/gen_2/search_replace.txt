<DESCRIPTION>
Raise the archive-relative success target as mean fitness improves. The current fixed target of 0.3 can keep increasing mutation width even near strong solutions, where smaller mutations may improve centroid performance. A fitness-dependent target preserves exploration at low returns and encourages gradual refinement at high returns, while retaining the existing decline-triggered reheating and persistent feedback. This uses observed fitness rather than a switch schedule; its performance remains to be evaluated.
