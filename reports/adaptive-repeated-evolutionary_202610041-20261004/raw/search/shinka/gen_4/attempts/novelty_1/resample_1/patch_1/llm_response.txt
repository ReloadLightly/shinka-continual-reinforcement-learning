archive_confirmed_reheating

<DESCRIPTION>
Require declining re-scored archive fitness to confirm reheating. A falling batch mean can result from overly broad mutations even while the archive remains strong; increasing sigma then can amplify the decline. Store smoothed archive fitness in the fourth memory slot, retaining its initialization role through a 1.0 offset. This preserves reheating when both population and archive performance deteriorate, using feedback without assuming a task-switch schedule.
</DESCRIPTION>
