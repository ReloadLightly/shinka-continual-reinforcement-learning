<DESCRIPTION>
Detect fitness drops using the archive’s own smoothed history instead of the whole-population mean. This makes exploration pulses respond more directly to deterioration in previously selected policies. A faster archive-history update shortens pulses once fitness stabilizes, allowing fine-grained search to resume. This is a feedback-based hypothesis; improvement requires evaluation.
</DESCRIPTION>
