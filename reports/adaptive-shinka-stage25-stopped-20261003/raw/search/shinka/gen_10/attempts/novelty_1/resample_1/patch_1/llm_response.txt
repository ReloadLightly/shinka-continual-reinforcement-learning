<DESCRIPTION>
A single strong individual can currently force mutation width down while the archive and population remain weak. Base difficulty on their average quality instead, and anchor width to a positive exploration target. Geometric interpolation should prevent persistent contraction from trapping search at the lower bound, while archive-loss feedback increases exploration after observed deterioration. These are feedback-driven changes; their effect on combined_score requires evaluation.
