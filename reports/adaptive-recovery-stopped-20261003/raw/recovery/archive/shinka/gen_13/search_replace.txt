<DESCRIPTION>
Anchor the mutation width to task difficulty and stalled progress, while retaining the existing probe feedback as a smaller correction. Best fitness reduces exploration when promising individuals already exist; low diversity and stalled progress increase exploration when performance remains poor. A quality decline temporarily raises the target. Smaller probes should reduce disruptive width oscillations and noisy gradient attribution. This is a proposed improvement, not a verified score gain.
